Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 18, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the NextStop mobile application ("App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our service. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Overview

NextStop is a train schedule and information app. The App is designed with your privacy in mind and currently does not collect any personal information.

Information We Collect

The App does not currently collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not require account creation, and we do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.

Internet and Network Usage

The App requires an internet connection to fetch train schedule data from remote servers. These network requests do not include any personal or identifying information. Standard technical data such as your IP address may be processed by the servers during these requests, but this data is not stored or used to identify you.

Analytics and Diagnostics

The App does not currently use any analytics or crash reporting services. We may incorporate services such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics in the future to help us understand how the App is used and to improve its performance. If we do, these services may collect anonymous, non-personally-identifiable usage data such as:

  • Device type, model, and operating system version
  • App usage patterns and feature interactions
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • General geographic region (country or city level, not precise location)

We will update this policy before enabling any such services.

User Accounts and Authentication

The App does not currently require or support user sign-in or account creation. If we introduce account features in the future, this policy will be updated to clearly describe what information is collected and how it is used.

Location Data

The App does not access or collect your device's location data.

Third-Party Services

The App currently does not integrate any third-party services that collect user data. If such services are integrated in the future, we will update this policy to list them and link to their respective privacy policies.

Data Sharing

Since we do not collect personal data, we do not share, sell, trade, or transfer any personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

The App does not collect or store any personal data, so there is no personal data retained. Any locally cached data (such as schedule information) is stored only on your device and can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the App.

Data Security

Although we do not collect personal information, we are committed to ensuring the App communicates securely with external servers using industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS).

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. If this changes in the future, we will provide mechanisms for you to exercise your data rights in accordance with applicable laws, including the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of data collection where applicable

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
